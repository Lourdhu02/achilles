## Where to find papers

- **arxiv.org** — search by title
- **Papers With Code** — implementations linked
- **Semantic Scholar** — references and citations
- **arxiv-sanity (Karpathy)** — better browsing
- **Hugging Face Papers** — daily new papers with discussion

## How to read efficiently

For each paper, 2 hours total:
1. Abstract + intro + conclusion (20 min)
2. Figures + tables skim (15 min)
3. One explainer blog or video (30 min)
4. Your 5-bullet summary (15 min)
5. Practice articulation in 90 sec (15 min)
6. Predict 3 follow-up Qs (15 min)
7. Spaced re-review in 1 week (10 min)

30 papers × 2 hrs = 60 hrs total. Spread over Months 5-8 = ~3 papers/week.

## Quick triage: read full vs skim vs skip

### Read full
- One of the 30 must-knows
- Specifically related to your current project
- Cited heavily by recent work you care about

### Skim (abstract + figures + conclusion only)
- Recent paper buzz but not in your top 30
- Background context for something you're reading

### Skip
- Application papers in domains far from yours
- Repetitive papers ("X but at Y scale")
- Press-release-grade announcements

## Newer papers (post-2024)

The frontier moves fast. Watch:

- **Reasoning models papers** (o1, R1, etc.)
- **Long-context techniques** (YaRN, position interpolation extensions)
- **MoE refinements** (DeepSeek-V3, etc.)
- **Agent benchmark papers** (SWE-Bench, GAIA, etc.)
- **Multimodal papers** (Gemini, GPT-4o variants)
- **Constitutional / Alignment** (Anthropic regularly publishes)

Tools to surface:
- Hugging Face daily papers
- The Gradient newsletter
- Sasha Rush's "Aria Notes"
- Lilian Weng blog (covers recent papers)
