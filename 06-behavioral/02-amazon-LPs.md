# Amazon Leadership Principles — Your Story Map

Amazon interviews are LP-driven. **Every interviewer asks 2-3 behavioral questions tied to specific LPs.** Failing to map your stories to LPs = guaranteed rejection.

For Amazon: prepare **2 stories per LP = 32 stories minimum.** Most candidates have 0-3. This is your edge.

---

## The 16 LPs

1. **Customer Obsession** — Start with the customer; work backwards
2. **Ownership** — Think long-term; act on behalf of the company, not just team
3. **Invent and Simplify** — New solutions; simplify complexity
4. **Are Right, A Lot** — Strong judgment; seek diverse perspectives
5. **Learn and Be Curious** — Never done learning
6. **Hire and Develop the Best** — Recognize talent; coach
7. **Insist on the Highest Standards** — High bar; deliver quality
8. **Think Big** — Bold direction; long-term thinking
9. **Bias for Action** — Speed matters; calculated risk
10. **Frugality** — Accomplish more with less
11. **Earn Trust** — Listen, communicate, be honest
12. **Dive Deep** — Stay connected to details; nothing is "beneath" you
13. **Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit** — Challenge respectfully; commit fully once decided
14. **Deliver Results** — Focus on key inputs; deliver on time
15. **Strive to be Earth's Best Employer** — Safer, productive, fair workplace
16. **Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility** — Better, do better for customers, employees, communities

- **Story B:** Advocating for a junior teammate at Sujanix.

### Broad Responsibility
- **Story A:** Privacy-first FinSentinelAI choices — long-term responsibility to customers' data and regulated environments.
- **Story B:** A decision where you prioritized long-term company/customer good over short-term gain.

## What Amazon interviewers ACTUALLY ask

Below are real examples of LP-specific questions. Practice answering each.

### Customer Obsession
- "Tell me about a time you went above and beyond for a customer."
- "Describe a time you used customer feedback to change your approach."
- "Tell me about a time you had to make a hard tradeoff between what the customer wanted and what was right."

### Ownership
- "Tell me about a time you took on something outside your job description."
- "Describe a time you saw something broken and fixed it, even though nobody asked."
- "Tell me about a long-term project you championed."

### Invent and Simplify
- "Walk through an innovation you came up with."
- "Tell me about a time you simplified a complex process."
- "What's the most original thing you've built?"

### Are Right, A Lot
- "Tell me about a decision you made with limited data."
- "Describe a time you changed your mind based on new information."
- "Tell me about a time you were wrong about a decision and how you recovered."

### Learn and Be Curious
- "Tell me about something new you learned in the last 6 months and applied at work."
- "Describe a time you had to learn a new technology to solve a problem."
- "What's your learning routine?"

### Hire and Develop the Best
- "Tell me about a hiring decision you made."
- "Describe how you've helped someone grow."
- "Tell me about a time you had to give difficult feedback."

### Insist on Highest Standards
- "Tell me about a quality bar you held."
- "Describe a time you refused to ship something."
- "Tell me about a time you raised standards for your team."

### Think Big
- "Describe a 2-year vision you proposed."
- "Tell me about an ambitious project that succeeded (or failed)."
- "What would you build if you had unlimited resources?"

### Bias for Action
- "Tell me about a time you took a risk that paid off."
- "Describe a time you had to act with incomplete information."
- "Tell me about a project you shipped fast."

### Frugality
- "Describe a time you delivered with significantly fewer resources."
- "Tell me about a cost reduction you led."
- "How do you balance quality with cost?"

### Earn Trust
- "Tell me about a time you had to win over a skeptical stakeholder."
- "Describe a time you delivered bad news."
- "How do you build trust with new teammates?"

### Dive Deep
- "Tell me about a time you found a bug others missed."
- "Describe a time you investigated something far beyond what was needed."
- "What's a technical detail you're proud of mastering?"

### Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit
- "Tell me about a disagreement with your manager."
- "Describe a time you pushed back on a decision you didn't agree with."
- "Tell me about a time you had to commit to a decision you didn't initially support."

### Deliver Results
- "Tell me about a tough deadline you delivered on."
- "Describe the impact of a project you led."
- "Walk me through your delivery process."

## Anti-patterns Amazon interviewers hate

- "We" without "I" (no clear ownership)
- Stories without numbers
- Stories without your specific decisions
- Stories that don't fit the LP being asked
- "Hypothetical" answers ("I would..." instead of "I did...")
- Defensive responses to follow-ups
- Bragging without humility
- Self-deprecation without recovery

## Drilling protocol

### Weeks 1-2 (Month 6 of overall plan)
- Develop 16 stories (1 per LP)
- Practice each out loud 3x

### Weeks 3-4
- Develop 16 more (2nd story per LP)
- Practice random selection

### Months 7-9
- Quiz yourself weekly
- Refine based on practice feedback
- By Month 9: 32 stories, each polished to 2-3 minute delivery

### Before any Amazon interview
- 1 hour: review all 16 LPs
- 1 hour: quick-rehearse 16 primary stories
- 30 min: read recent Amazon news / strategy / product launches
