# "Why This Company?" — Per-Target Answers

The most-asked behavioral question. **Generic answers kill interviews.** Specific, researched answers win them.

A great "Why X?" has 3 components:
1. **What's specific to THEIR work** — a product, team, paper, or philosophy you genuinely engage with
2. **What's specific to YOU** — why this matches YOUR background and goals
3. **A specific bet on the future** — what you'd want to contribute

---

## Template for any company

```
"I've been following [Company]'s work on [specific product / team / research direction]. 
What draws me specifically is [specific insight or angle].

Given my work on [your project / experience that maps], I see a strong fit because [reason].

If I joined, I'd want to contribute to [specific team / problem area] because [reason]."
```

## How to use these

These are STARTING POINTS. Before any actual interview:

1. Re-read 2-3 of their recent blog posts / paper / product launches
2. Update the answer with the latest specific detail
3. Practice OUT LOUD until natural
4. Adapt to specific team if you know which team

---

## Anti-patterns

### "Why X?" answers that fail
- "Great compensation and brand" (transactional)
- "Cutting-edge AI work" (generic)
- "I want to learn from the best" (passive)
- "Friends work there" (not differentiated)
- Anything that could apply to 5 other companies

### Good "Why X?" markers
- Cites specific products, papers, teams
- Connects to specific things YOU'VE done
- Shows you understand the business + technical direction
- Has a forward-looking bet (where you'd contribute)
