You are turning **svtrv2** (https://github.com/Lourdhu02/svtrv2) into a submitted paper. The repo reproduces **SVTRv2** (Du et al., "SVTRv2: CTC Beats Encoder-Decoder Models in Scene Text Recognition", ICCV 2025; PDF in `papers/`) and proposes **ARD (Adaptive Routing and Semantic-Guidance Distillation)**, an extension that leaves inference unchanged (`research/method/method.md`):
- **Adaptive MSR routing (`--route`):** a small learned router replaces the hand-set aspect-ratio buckets, trained with a Bradley–Terry preference loss over which canvas the recognizer reads best.
- **SGM → CTC distillation (`--distill`):** the train-only semantic guidance module becomes a soft teacher for the CTC head under uniform or Viterbi alignment. The exported model is byte-identical to the baseline.
