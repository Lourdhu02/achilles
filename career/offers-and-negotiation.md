# Offers and negotiation

1. **No leverage until the offer exists.** Don't negotiate during interviews; don't name numbers early. "I'm focused on fit; I'm confident we'll find a fair number."
2. **Compare total compensation:** base + bonus + equity per year (check the vesting schedule; some are back-loaded) + sign-on + refreshers. Get the written breakdown.
3. **Competing offers are the real leverage.** Run parallel pipelines. Never invent an offer.
4. **Script:** thank them → "I'm excited about {team}. To commit, I'm looking for total compensation closer to {X}, based on {competing offer / market data}. Is there flexibility in base, equity or sign-on?" → silence → if they close the gap: "If you can do {X}, I'll sign today," and mean it.
5. **India specifics:** the joining bonus is often the most flexible component; a notice-period buyout can be negotiated; get remote or hybrid terms and relocation in writing; for startups, understand ESOP strike price, vesting and liquidity.
6. **Walk away** from exploding offers under ~48 hours, refusals to put terms in writing, or hostility.
