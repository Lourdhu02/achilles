# Career — the job-search machinery, condensed

Condensed from the earlier playbook (the full original is in git history, commit `de97e92`). Two principles:
1. **Honesty is a strategy.** Every claim will be probed by people who know more than you. Understate and over-deliver.
2. **Durable vs volatile.** Advice here is durable. Company-specific facts, interview formats, compensation and visa rules change, so they live in [market-intel.md](market-intel.md) and [visa-and-relocation.md](visa-and-relocation.md), dated, to be re-verified.
