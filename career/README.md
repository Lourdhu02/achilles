# Career: the job-search machinery

How to turn the work in this repo into interviews and offers at core-AI teams (or into credibility for the founder track): the résumé, stories, outreach, the application funnel, negotiation, public proof of work, and routines that keep you going for a year.
Condensed from an earlier, longer playbook (the full original is in git history, commit `de97e92`), then rebuilt around templates and scripts you can use directly.

## Principles

1. **Honesty is a strategy.** Every claim will be probed by people who know more than you. Use only numbers you can source from your own records, understate, and over-deliver. The résumé guide and story bank flag the numbers that still need checking.
2. **Evidence over prediction.** This module gives no invented probabilities of getting hired. Track leading indicators instead ([profile.md](profile.md#leading-indicators-track-these-no-invented-probabilities)) and change the plan when they stall.
3. **Durable vs volatile.** Advice here is durable. Company-specific facts (teams, interview formats, compensation, visa rules) change, so they live in dated places: the [company guides](../companies/README.md), [market-intel.md](market-intel.md) and [visa-and-relocation.md](visa-and-relocation.md). Re-verify before acting.
4. **Proof of work beats claims.** A linked lab, a write-up with error bars and a merged pull request persuade more than any adjective on a résumé.

> [!TIP]
> Spend career time in proportion to the stage. Before May 2027, about 2 hours a week (one relationship, one piece of public work) is enough; the labs are the main job. From May, applications and mocks take a larger share, as the roadmap describes.
