# Market intel: targets, teams, formats

**Volatile. Last reviewed September 2026. Re-verify quarterly on careers pages, engineering blogs, Levels.fyi and conversations with employees.**

## Target map by track
- **Frontier labs (core research engineering):** Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta (superintelligence / Llama teams), xAI, Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen/Alibaba, Moonshot. Most core roles are in SF, London or Paris; some are remote. The bar is global.
- **Core-ML at Big Tech with India presence:** Google (DeepMind India, Gemini/Cloud AI), Microsoft (Microsoft AI, Azure AI, MSR India), NVIDIA (inference, TensorRT-LLM, applied research; Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad), Meta, Amazon (AGI and Bedrock), Adobe (Firefly), Apple (on-device models).
- **Infra and model companies:** Databricks (Mosaic AI), Hugging Face (remote; OSS-first hiring), Together AI, Fireworks, Modal, Cohere, Scale AI.
- **India AI builders:** Sarvam AI, Krutrim, and IIT/IISc-linked research groups: natural homes for Indic-language work.
- **Startups (founder-track learning):** early-stage AI application companies where you own a whole system.

## Formats (as generally reported; confirm per company)
Frontier labs lean on practical coding, ML deep dives, system design and values rounds, with less LeetCode. Big Tech still runs DSA rounds plus ML design. Amazon maps every round to its Leadership Principles, so prepare stories accordingly. Meta and Google probe past projects deeply; every résumé claim gets tested.
