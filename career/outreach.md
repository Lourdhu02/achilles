# Outreach: referrals and cold contact

**Principle:** make it easy and low-risk for someone to vouch for you. That means legible public work (labs, write-ups, PRs) and a specific ask.

## Build the network before you need it
Engage with people's public work for weeks (thoughtful comments, reproducing their result, a PR to their repo). Contribute in communities (GPU MODE, EleutherAI, HF, local meetups). Aim for ~1 genuine new relationship per week; after a year that is ~50 people who know your work.

## Templates (adapt; keep them under 120 words)
**First contact**
> Hi {name}, I read your {post/paper/PR} on {topic}. I reproduced {specific result} on a consumer GPU and found {one concrete observation} ({link}). Curious whether you saw {specific question}. No ask beyond that; thanks for publishing it.

**After a conversation, asking for a referral**
> Thanks again for the chat about {topic}. I'm applying to {exact role, link} on {team}. Given {the relevant work you know I've done}, would you be comfortable referring me? Here's a 3-line summary you can paste: {summary}. Totally fine if not.

**Via a mutual contact**
> Hi {mutual}, would you be open to introducing me to {target}? I'm working on {specific overlap} and would value 15 minutes of their perspective on {question}. Happy to send a blurb you can forward.

**Cold to a hiring manager**
> {One line on a specific thing their team shipped} → {one line of evidence you can do that work: a link to your artifact} → {one line of ask: "Is {role} still open, and is this the kind of background you're looking for?"}

## Don'ts
"Please refer me for any role"; résumé attachments in the first message; the same message to ten people at one company; more than one follow-up.
