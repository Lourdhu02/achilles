# Visa and relocation

**Volatile. Written September 2026. Verify every rule with official sources and an immigration lawyer before deciding.**

- **Most reliable path to the US:** join a multinational in India, then transfer internally after ~1 year (L-1). It is slow but has no lottery.
- **Direct US hire:** usually H-1B, which is lottery-based. The rules changed substantially in 2025 (a large fee on certain new petitions, and moves toward wage-weighted selection); check the current state before counting on it.
- **O-1:** "extraordinary ability". Publications, significant OSS, awards and press are the evidence. The research and portfolio plan builds exactly that evidence over time.
- **UK:** Skilled Worker visa (employer-sponsored, no lottery) and the Global Talent visa (self-petitioned; check the current endorsement route). Several frontier labs have London offices.
- **EU:** EU Blue Card and national skilled-worker schemes (Germany, Netherlands, France). Paris hosts several AI labs.
- **Remote from India** for global companies: growing, but often restricted to specific countries. Confirm it in the offer.

Keep your passport valid, save a relocation buffer, and ask recruiters early: "Does this role sponsor visas, and what's the typical timeline?"
