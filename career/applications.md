# Applications: the funnel

## Run it like an experiment
Track every application; debug the funnel by its stage:
| symptom | likely cause | fix |
|---|---|---|
| many applications, no callbacks | résumé or targeting | sharper résumé, referrals, role fit |
| callbacks, no technical screens pass | coding speed or fundamentals | timed drills, mocks |
| screens pass, onsites fail | depth, system design, deep dive | question bank, mocks, write-ups |
| onsites pass, no offers | leveling, values fit, competition | debrief with recruiters; target better-fit teams |

Apply in waves: lower-stakes targets first as practice, dream targets once your mocks pass consistently. Parallel pipelines create offer overlap, which gives you negotiation leverage.

## Interview debrief (within 24 h of every round)
Round type · questions asked (verbatim as best you can) · where I was strong · where I stalled · what I'll drill this week · did I ask good questions? · thank-you sent?

## Timing
Many companies hire year-round; budgets often open at the start of a quarter or fiscal year. Apply within days of a posting. For India roles, plan for 30–90 day notice periods and say so early.
