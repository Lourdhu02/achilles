# Sustainability

- **Weekly review** (Sunday, 45 min): [journal/templates/weekly-review.md](../journal/templates/weekly-review.md). Plan with 20% slack.
- **Monthly review:** [journal/templates/monthly-review.md](../journal/templates/monthly-review.md). Adjust the roadmap to your actual pace; don't pretend.
- **Non-negotiables:** 7+ hours of sleep, exercise 3×/week, one full day off every other week, time with people you care about. Output quality falls off a cliff before you notice it's happening.
- **When stuck or demotivated:** shrink the task to 25 minutes, finish something small, write down what blocked you. After two stuck weeks, ask for help (communities, a mentor, a study partner).
- **Keep your job** while preparing unless you have a runway and a reason; financial stress makes bad decisions.
