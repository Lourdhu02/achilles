## Pre-interview ritual checklist (copy + fill per interview)

**Interview: [Company] [Role] [Date]**

24 hours before:
- [ ] Re-read company "Why [X]?" prep
- [ ] Re-read relevant role JD
- [ ] Re-skim my STAR stories most relevant to this round
- [ ] Re-skim relevant technical deep-dive file
- [ ] Confirm logistics (link works, ID ready, location/timezone)
- [ ] Get good sleep

2 hours before:
- [ ] Eat moderately
- [ ] No caffeine if it makes me jittery
- [ ] 10-min review of last week's mock notes
- [ ] Quick rehearsal of "tell me about yourself"
- [ ] Open relevant docs, tabs ready

10 min before:
- [ ] Bathroom break
- [ ] Water at desk
- [ ] Camera + mic test
- [ ] Take 3 deep breaths
- [ ] Open the calendar invite, click link

Right after:
- [ ] Write 5-minute brain dump of what happened
- [ ] Note questions you bombed → schedule deeper study
- [ ] Update tracker
- [ ] Send thank-you email to recruiter within 24 hours

## Templates: emails to recruiters

### Post-interview thank-you (within 24 hours)
```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Thanks for setting up today's interview with [Interviewer Name]. I really enjoyed 
discussing [specific topic from the interview] and learning more about the [team / 
role / company].

Looking forward to the next steps. Please let me know if there's anything else I can 
provide on my end.

Best,
Lourdu
```

### Follow-up if no response after 1 week
```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Wanted to check in on the timeline for the [role] process. Last we spoke, the next 
step would be [X]. Is there anything I should provide on my end?

Thanks,
Lourdu
```

### Politely declining an offer
```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Thanks again for the offer for [role]. After significant reflection, I've decided to 
go in a different direction at this time. I really appreciated my conversations with 
your team and have a lot of respect for [Company].

I hope our paths cross again in the future.

Best,
Lourdu
```

### Accepting an offer
```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Thrilled to confirm — I'm accepting the offer for [role] at [Company]. Looking 
forward to joining [Date].

Please let me know the next steps for onboarding.

Best,
Lourdu
```
