## The mental model

**Job applications are a funnel, not a ceremony.**

- Volume matters (more applications = more chances)
- Quality matters (good applications convert at 10x rates)
- Variety matters (League A + B, multiple roles per company)
- Velocity matters (parallel pipelines accelerate everything)

Optimize for OUTPUT (offers), not for INPUTS (resumes sent).

## The 80/20 of getting offers

20% of effort produces 80% of offers:

1. **Referrals** — 5-10x conversion vs cold apply
2. **Quality of resume** — gating for everything
3. **Practice mocks** — actual interview prep
4. **Following up + persistence** — most candidates ghost or give up

If you do these four well, you get offers. If you skip them, no amount of LeetCode saves you.
