# Cold Outreach — Direct to Hiring Managers + Recruiters

When you don't have a referral, can you skip the ATS by going direct? Sometimes yes.

---

## When cold outreach works

- The role isn't yet posted (insider hiring)
- The hiring manager is active on LinkedIn / Twitter
- You have a uniquely strong angle (matches their recent post / paper / project)
- You bring SPECIFIC value (not just "looking for jobs")

## When cold outreach fails

- Generic "hire me" messages
- HM has 1000+ LinkedIn requests (top FAANG managers do)
- Your background doesn't match what they're hiring for
- You're impatient and follow up aggressively

## Template 1: Cold to hiring manager — specific job posting

```
Hi [Manager Name],

Saw the [role] opening on [Company]'s careers page. The work on [specific thing the 
team does] caught my attention — particularly because of [specific connection to your 
work].

I'm Lourdu, ML Engineer at Sujanix in Bengaluru. Currently architecting Transformer-
based OCR pipelines. Previously founded SpaceDrift, where I delivered 12+ international 
GenAI/CV systems for regulated customers.

Two of my projects directly map to what your team is working on:
- [Project relevant to their work] — github.com/Lourdhu02/[repo]
- [Project relevant to their work] — github.com/Lourdhu02/[repo]

If you're considering candidates from outside the typical pipeline, I'd love a 15-min 
conversation. Happy to provide additional details or interview at your convenience.

Resume: [link]

Lourdu Raju
linkedin.com/in/lourdhu | github.com/Lourdhu02
```

**Response rate:** 5-15% (low absolute, high quality when it works).

## Template 2: Cold to recruiter on LinkedIn

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Following [Company]'s recent [news / launch / product update], I'm specifically 
interested in the [team / role area].

I'm a Bengaluru-based ML Engineer with 2 years building production GenAI and CV 
systems. My background:
- Current: ML Engineer at Sujanix, OCR pipelines (97% production accuracy)
- Previous: Founded SpaceDrift (MSME sole proprietorship, 16 months solo delivery across freelance, engineering, and PhD-scholar research support)
- Notable: Kaggle Expert; ECHOME (agentic AI with novel memory); FinSentinelAI 
  (production enterprise RAG)

Specifically interested in roles related to [specific team / work area]. Are there 
open positions you're sourcing for?

Happy to send formal application + resume.

Lourdu | LinkedIn | GitHub
```

**Response rate:** 20-30% if you target recruiters who actively post about openings.

## Template 3: Cold for a role that doesn't exist yet

For your dream company where you don't see a posted role:

```
Hi [Hiring Manager / Senior Engineer],

I've been following [Company]'s work on [specific area — e.g., Gemini Apps, Bedrock 
Agents]. Specifically, [recent technical detail you genuinely engaged with].

While I don't see a posted role matching my profile exactly, I wondered if your team 
might be growing in this area. I bring:
- Production GenAI experience (ECHOME, FinSentinelAI)
- 2 YOE shipping ML to real users
- Strong system design + LLM internals understanding

If you'd be open to a brief conversation OR can point me to the right person at 
[Company], I'd be grateful.

Lourdu Raju
```

**Response rate:** Low (5-10%) but occasionally yields a hidden opportunity.

## Template 4: Cold to someone who wrote a paper / blog post you admire

```
Hi [Author],

I read your post / paper "[Title]" recently. The argument about [specific point] 
clarified my thinking on [related problem in your work].

I'm exploring this area myself — I just published [your related work] which builds 
on a similar idea but [different angle].

If you'd be open to a 20-min conversation, I'd value your perspective. Either way, 
thanks for the writing — it had real impact on me.

Lourdu | [your post link] | [your GitHub]
```

**This is genuine relationship building.** Not pure outreach.

## Where to find hiring manager contacts

### LinkedIn
- Search: "[Role Title] [Company] [City]"
- Filter by "1st" or "2nd" connections
- Look for senior people (Staff, Principal, Director)

### Twitter/X
- Many hiring managers post about openings
- Follow ML influencers from target companies

### Conferences
- Track speakers from target companies
- DM after conferences referencing their talks

### Cold email
- Most company directories give email patterns (e.g., firstname.lastname@google.com)
- Verify with Hunter.io or similar
- Use professional, NOT spammy

---

## The follow-up rule

After cold outreach:
- Wait 7-10 days
- Send 1 follow-up (max)
- After that, move on

Multiple follow-ups damage your reputation more than help.

---

## Quality bar (before sending cold)

Before hitting send on a cold message, check:
- [ ] Specific name (not "to whom it may concern")
- [ ] Specific company / team reference
- [ ] Specific reason you're contacting THEM (not just any senior person)
- [ ] Specific match between your work + their needs
- [ ] Concrete ask (15 min call, referral, info)
- [ ] Easy to decline (no pressure)
- [ ] Provides credibility links
- [ ] Spell-checked, professional, signed

If a check is missing: don't send. Polish first.

## When cold outreach is your ONLY option

For roles where:
- You don't know anyone at the company
- The role is competitive (hundreds of applicants)
- Your profile is solid but not exceptional

Cold outreach + a great message has converted L4-L5 candidates at major FAANG companies. Don't dismiss it.

But: it's a 5-15% conversion path. Use it IN ADDITION TO ATS apply + referral attempts, not INSTEAD OF.
