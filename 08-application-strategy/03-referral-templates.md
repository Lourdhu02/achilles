# Referral Outreach Templates

Use, adapt, never copy verbatim.

For full strategy: `01-hiring-process/05-the-referral-game.md`.

---

## Template 1: First-touch cold LinkedIn message (warm)

```
Subject (LinkedIn auto-fills): Connecting

Hi [Name],

I came across your work at [Company]. As someone building [your domain — agentic AI / 
production RAG / etc.] at Sujanix, your recent [post / talk / project] on [specific 
topic] resonated.

I'm Lourdu — ML Engineer based in Bengaluru, currently focused on production GenAI 
systems. Connecting to follow more of your work.

If you'd be open to a 15-min conversation about [their team / their domain / their work], 
I'd genuinely value the perspective.

Lourdu | LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/lourdhu | GitHub: github.com/Lourdhu02
```

**Why this works:**
- Specific to them (not template-y)
- Doesn't mention referral
- Low-effort ask (15 min, optional)
- Provides credibility links

**Acceptance rate:** ~30-50% if note is genuine and their work is genuinely interesting to you.

---

## Template 2: After they accept, light engagement (NO ask yet)

Wait 1-2 weeks after they accept. Engage with their content. Then:

```
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!

I noticed your post on [topic]. The part about [specific point] particularly stood 
out to me because [your relevant experience or thought].

I've been thinking about [related problem] in my own work on [your project]. Curious 
how your team thinks about [specific question]?

No need to write a long reply — even a one-liner is great.

Lourdu
```

**Why this works:**
- Specific engagement
- Asks an OPEN question (not just "thoughts?")
- Easy to reply to

---

## Template 3: Asking for a coffee/call (after warming)

After 2-3 message exchanges or genuine engagement:

```
Hi [Name],

Really appreciated [recent exchange topic]. Your perspective on [X] is helping me 
think through [related thing in my work].

Would you be open to a 20-min call sometime in the next 2-3 weeks? I'd love to hear 
more about your work at [Company] and what your team is working on.

Happy to send a calendar link or work around your timezone.

Lourdu
```

**Why this works:**
- Acknowledges relationship
- Concrete time bounds
- Reciprocal framing (you'll learn, but they share)
- Their time is respected

**Conversion:** ~30-50% to actual call.

---

## Template 4: On the call (script outline)

Goals: be human, get information, build relationship. NOT to ask for referral.

```
- 5 min: small talk, intros, your background briefly
- 10 min: ask THEM questions (their work, team, challenges)
- 3 min: share YOUR work briefly when natural
- 2 min: thank, mention you'd love to stay in touch
```

**Questions to ask THEM:**
- "What's a problem your team is solving right now that you find interesting?"
- "What kind of engineer thrives on your team?"
- "What's the most fun part of working there? Hardest?"
- "If I were considering [Company], what should I know that's not on the website?"

**DON'T:**
- Ask for referral on first call
- Pitch yourself heavily
- Talk about salary
- Sound transactional

## Template 5: After the call (follow-up + soft ask)

Within 24 hours:

```
[Name],

Thanks so much for the chat earlier. Really enjoyed hearing about [specific topic 
from conversation].

You mentioned [specific thing — e.g., "team is hiring across X area"]. I'm actively 
exploring opportunities and am very interested in [Company] — particularly the 
[specific team or work].

If you ever feel like the timing is right and you'd be comfortable making a referral, 
I'd be very grateful. Totally understand if not — happy to keep in touch regardless.

For your reference: 
- Resume: [link to your PDF or LinkedIn]
- GitHub: github.com/Lourdhu02
- Key projects: ECHOME (agentic AI), FinSentinelAI (enterprise RAG)

Thanks again!
Lourdu
```

**Why this works:**
- Soft ask with explicit out
- Provides all materials they'd need
- Honors the relationship even if no referral

## Template 6: Cold direct referral request (only if relationship is strong)

Skip steps 1-5 if you have a real existing relationship:

```
Hi [Name],

Hope all's well at [Company].

I'm applying to [specific role — link the JD]. Given our [shared background / past 
collaboration / etc.], I wondered if you'd be comfortable submitting a referral?

It would only take ~5 min through your internal referral portal. I've attached my 
resume and a brief paragraph about why this role fits.

If timing or fit isn't right, no worries — appreciate you considering.

Lourdu
[Attachments: resume, 1-paragraph fit summary]
```

Only use if you've actually worked with them or have a genuine prior relationship.

## Template 7: Asking via a mutual connection

```
Hi [Mutual],

Hope things are good!

Quick ask: I'm exploring opportunities at [Company] and I noticed you're connected to 
[Target Referrer]. I've engaged with their work on [topic] and would love to have a 
brief conversation about their team.

Would you be open to making a brief intro? Happy to provide context on what I'd want 
to discuss — totally fine if not the right fit or timing.

Thanks,
Lourdu
```

## Template 8: After getting referred but no callback (2-week follow-up)

```
Hi [Name],

Wanted to follow up — really appreciated your referral for [role] at [Company]. 
Hadn't heard back from the recruiter yet (it's been 2 weeks).

Just checking: is there anything else you'd suggest I do? I've also reached out to 
[recruiter name] directly, but no response.

Thanks again for the support!

Lourdu
```

---

## Template 9: After interview rejection (maintain relationship)

```
Hi [Name],

Wanted to let you know — got the news that [Company] decided not to move forward 
with my candidacy for [role]. While I'm disappointed, the process was great learning.

Really appreciate you supporting my application. I genuinely enjoyed our conversation 
about [topic] and look forward to staying in touch.

If you have any feedback you'd be willing to share (recruiter or otherwise mentioned 
anything), I'd value it.

Either way, thanks again!

Lourdu
```

**Why this matters:** Grace in rejection is remembered. Future relationships preserve.

## Anti-patterns to avoid

### "Hi sir/madam, I'm looking for opportunities at your company, please refer me"
- Generic
- Demanding
- No relationship
- Will be ignored

### Copy-pasting same message to 10 people
- LinkedIn often shows "X people also got this message"
- Embarrassing
- Devalues you

### Sending resume in first message
- Premature
- Skips the relationship
- Most people delete

### Asking for "any" role
- Vague
- Signals desperation
- Lower-quality candidates

### Following up 3+ times if no response
- Annoying
- Damages relationship
- 1 follow-up max

## Volume guidance

| Activity | Per week | Per month |
|---|---|---|
| New connections sent | 10-20 | 40-80 |
| Engagement on others' content | 5-10/day | ~20+ per week |
| Coffee chats requested | 2-3 | 8-12 |
| Coffee chats actually completed | 1-2 | 4-8 |
| Referral asks sent | 1-2 | 4-6 |

Spread over Months 3-12 = ~50-100 calls + ~40-50 referral asks total. Plenty.
