## The one meta-insight

> **The hiring process is not a meritocracy. It is a noisy filter optimized for risk avoidance.**

The best engineer in a room often doesn't get the offer. The candidate who:
- Has a referral (skips ATS noise)
- Communicates clearly (low-risk to interviewers)
- Shows up "professionally" in writing and verbally (signals "won't embarrass us")
- Knows the company's specific quirks (signals genuine interest)
- Negotiates calmly (signals confidence, not desperation)

...wins. Skill matters, but *legibility of skill* matters more. This folder is about making your skill legible.
