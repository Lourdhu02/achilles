# Track — Research engineer at a frontier lab

The goal: be hireable as a Member of Technical Staff / Research Engineer on a **core** team (pretraining, post-training/RL, inference, evals, interpretability) at an AI lab, or on the equivalent core-ML teams at Google DeepMind, Meta, NVIDIA, Microsoft AI and similar.

## The teams and what they test
| team | the work | what proves you can do it |
|---|---|---|
| Pretraining | data pipelines, architecture, scaling laws, large-run stability | labs 04–05, 08–09; a scaling-law sweep; a data-filtering study |
| Post-training / RL | SFT, preference optimization, RLVR, reward models, RL infrastructure | labs 10–12; GRPO on a small model with an honest write-up |
| Inference / performance | kernels, serving, quantization, speculative decoding | labs 06–07, 13–14; a Triton kernel with benchmarks; a vLLM/SGLang PR |
| Evals | benchmark design, statistics, harnesses | lab 15; a public leaderboard re-analysis with CIs |
| Interpretability / safety | circuits, SAEs, red teaming, safety evals | lab 16; an SAE study; a clear safety position |
| Applied / forward-deployed | customer-facing systems on frontier models | module 10; measured RAG and agent systems |

## Signal ladder (climb it in order)
1. Uses APIs → 2. **built it from scratch** (labs) → 3. **measured it and predicted the numbers** → 4. **reproduced a paper** → 5. **new result or merged OSS contribution** → 6. others use or cite your work.
This repo gets you to rung 3 by construction. The [portfolio plan](portfolio.md) gets you to 4–5.
