# 07 — Team and operating cadence

## Co-founder
A technical founder often benefits most from a GTM co-founder who owns selling. Test fit by working together on a real paid pilot for 1–3 months before committing. Put equity, roles, vesting and a decision process in writing.

## Hiring
First hires: people who ship end to end and talk to customers. Contractors are fine for bounded work (you learned this at SpaceDrift); keep the core (evals, data pipeline, key models) in-house. Hire for the ability to write clearly and measure honestly.

## Weekly operating rhythm
- Monday: metrics review (usage, retention, pipeline, burn) plus the top three priorities.
- Daily: ship something; one customer touchpoint.
- Friday: a written update to yourself, your advisors and later investors: what shipped, what we learned, what's next, what's blocked.
- Monthly: a runway and "default alive" check; kill or double down on experiments.

## Focus
Say no to custom work that doesn't generalize (the services trap), to shiny model tweaks the evals don't need, and to conferences before you have customers.
