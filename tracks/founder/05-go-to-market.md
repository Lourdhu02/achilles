# 05 — Go-to-market

## First 10 customers: founder-led sales
Define the **ICP** (industry, size, role, trigger event). Build a list of 100 target accounts. Reach out personally with a specific observation about their workflow. Offer a **paid pilot** with written success criteria (e.g. "90% of invoices extracted with no human edits within 30 days"), a price, and a conversion path. Run weekly check-ins; you personally watch users work.

## India vs global
Indian enterprise and government: long cycles, procurement, relationships, and a strong preference for on-prem or local deployment. Your Sujanix exposure is directly useful here. Global SMB and mid-market: faster, self-serve and content-led, priced in USD. Many Indian AI founders build in India for global customers. Pick one motion first; don't run both at once.

## Technical founder's growth engine
Public writing and open source: benchmarks, eval write-ups, a free tool that solves a slice of the problem. It builds trust with technical buyers and attracts hires. Your research write-ups double as marketing.

## Metrics that prove product-market fit
Retention and usage depth first (cohorts), then expansion (net dollar retention), then organic pull (referrals, inbound). Revenue from pilots that don't convert is not PMF.
