## When to deviate from this plan

### Slow down if:
- You can't solve more than 50% of a pattern's problems without hints by end of week
- Solution time isn't dropping week-to-week within the pattern
- You feel like you're memorizing, not understanding

→ Add 1 week to the pattern. It's fine.

### Speed up if:
- You're solving everything well-within time limits
- You're getting bored
- You've genuinely mastered the pattern

→ Move ahead, add harder problems (LC-hards) to mixed practice.

## When you fail or feel stuck

This will happen. Universally. Here's the protocol:

**On a single problem (stuck >60 min):**
1. Read first hint
2. Try 15 more min
3. Read solution editorial
4. Identify: what pattern? What detail did I miss?
5. Close solution. Code from scratch.
6. Mark for review in 3 days.

**On a whole pattern (week ending without mastery):**
1. Don't move on. Add 1 week.
2. Watch NeetCode video for 2 hardest unsolved problems in that pattern.
3. Solve 5 more problems in that pattern.
4. THEN move on.

**On the entire plan (feeling overwhelmed, want to quit):**
1. Take a 2-day break. Real break, no LC.
2. Open `00-START-HERE/01-honest-truth.md`. Re-read.
3. Talk to me (Claude). Tell me what's blocking you.
4. Resume with the SMALLEST possible step. Just 1 problem.
5. Build momentum back.

This is normal. Everyone hits the wall at week 4-6 (graphs) and week 10-11 (DP). Plan for it.
