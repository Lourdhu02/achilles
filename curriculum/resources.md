# Resources (curated: fewer, better)

## Courses, in the order to take them
1. **Karpathy, *Neural Networks: Zero to Hero*** and *Let's reproduce GPT-2 (124M)*: the build-from-scratch spine.
2. **Stanford CS336, *Language Modeling from Scratch***: the closest thing to this curriculum at university level. Do its assignments alongside labs 04–09.
3. **GPU MODE** lectures and Discord: kernels, Triton, CUDA ([lab 06](../labs/06_attention_kernels/README.md)).
4. **ARENA**: mechanistic interpretability and RL for LLMs, free and hands-on.
5. **OpenAI *Spinning Up in Deep RL*** plus Sutton & Barto (chapters 1–6 and 13) before [module 06](06-post-training.md).
6. MIT 6.5940 (Song Han, *Efficient ML*): quantization, pruning, efficient inference.

## Books
- *Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)*, Raschka (2024): gentle companion to labs 04–05.
- *Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts*, Bishop & Bishop (2023): rigorous reference.
- *AI Engineering*, Chip Huyen (2025): applied systems and founder track.
- *Designing Machine Learning Systems*, Chip Huyen (2022): ML system design interviews.
- *Programming Massively Parallel Processors*, Hwu, Kirk & El Hajj: CUDA fundamentals.
- *The RLHF Book*, Nathan Lambert (free online).
- *Mathematics for Machine Learning*, Deisenroth et al. (free).

## Long-form writing worth rereading
Horace He, *Making Deep Learning Go Brrrr*; kipply, *Transformer Inference Arithmetic*; Google DeepMind, *How to Scale Your Model*; Hugging Face, *Ultra-Scale Playbook*; Lilian Weng's blog; Karpathy's *A Recipe for Training Neural Networks*; Simon Boehm, *How to Optimize a CUDA Matmul Kernel*; Anthropic's *Building Effective Agents*.

## Communities (contribute, don't just lurk)
GPU MODE Discord · EleutherAI Discord · Hugging Face forums and Discord · r/LocalLLaMA · Latent Space · Bengaluru meetups and AI Tinkerers. Answer questions there after each lab; that is where referrals start.

## Anti-list
Skip "master AI in 30 days" content, framework-of-the-month tutorials, and prompt-engineering certificates. If a resource doesn't make you build or derive something, it's entertainment.
