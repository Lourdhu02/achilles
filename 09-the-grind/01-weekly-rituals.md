# Weekly Rituals — The Sunday Review

60 minutes every Sunday. Non-negotiable. This single hour saves 10 hours of drift during the week.

---

## The Sunday template (do this every Sunday evening)

### 1. Review the past week (15 min)

Open your hour log. Answer:

**Quantitative:**
- Hours logged: __ (vs target of ~25)
- DSA problems solved: __
- Topics studied: __
- Public outputs (blog, OSS PR, post): __
- Mocks completed: __

**Qualitative:**
- What went well this week?
- What didn't go well?
- What did I avoid? (red flag — that's probably where I need more focus)
- One specific learning from this week?

Write 3-5 honest sentences. Don't sugarcoat.

### 2. Plan the upcoming week (30 min)

Open `00-START-HERE/04-15-month-roadmap.md`. Check current month's tasks.

For the next 7 days, plan:

```markdown
## Week of [Date]

### Mon-Tue-Wed-Thu-Fri (2-3 hr each)
- Mon: [Specific task — e.g., "LC 4 problems on graphs"]
- Tue: [Specific task — e.g., "Read RAG deep dive + write summary"]
- Wed: [Specific task]
- Thu: [Specific task]
- Fri: [Specific task or rest]

### Saturday (4-8 hrs deep work)
- Morning: [Heavy lifting task — e.g., "1 ML system design problem timed"]
- Afternoon: [Project work — e.g., "Flagship #1 — implement eval module"]
- Evening: [Review + light tasks]

### Sunday (4-8 hrs)
- Morning: [Mock interview / Pramp]
- Afternoon: [Blog writing OR project polish]
- Evening: Sunday review ritual (this!)
```

Specific tasks > generic "study ML."

### 3. Stretch goals (5 min)

Pick 1-2 stretch goals for the week. These are bonus IF you have extra time:
- "Write a draft of next blog post"
- "Engage with 5 LinkedIn posts from target company engineers"
- "Read 1 paper from must-read list"

### 4. Calibrate vs the 15-month plan (10 min)

Open `00-START-HERE/04-15-month-roadmap.md`:
- Where in the roadmap am I supposed to be?
- Am I on track? Behind? Ahead?
- If behind, what's slipping? Is it temporary or structural?
- Adjust expectations: this week's focus should drag me toward the milestone

If you're behind by 1-2 weeks: catch up in the next 2 weeks via slight intensity boost.

If you're behind by 1+ months: time to re-baseline the roadmap honestly. Push the apply window back if needed. Don't pretend.

## Weekday rituals (10 min each, daily)

### Morning (5 min)
- Look at today's plan from Sunday
- Pick the SINGLE most important task for today
- Block 1-2 hours for it

### Evening (5 min)
- Log hours in tracker
- Note: what did I actually finish? what didn't I get to?
- Quick mood check: am I feeling burned out? excited? blocked?

## Saturday + Sunday deep-work patterns

### Saturday morning (4 hours)
Best window for hardest work. Use it for:
- Mock interviews
- System design problems
- New DSA patterns
- Project deep work

### Saturday afternoon (3-4 hours)
- Project building (less mental cost than learning new patterns)
- Code cleanup, README polish
- Re-reviewing past problems

### Sunday morning (3-4 hours)
- Reading / research
- Blog drafting
- Mock interviews (Pramp peers often free on weekends)

### Sunday afternoon
- Lighter work
- LinkedIn engagement
- Review

### Sunday evening (1 hour)
- The ritual above

## When you blow your weekly plan

Inevitable. Don't catastrophize.

**Response protocol:**
1. Did 50%+ of plan? → You're fine. Adjust next week's plan to absorb residual.
2. Did 25-50% of plan? → Real backslide. Identify root cause (life, motivation, illness, bad estimation).
3. Did <25%? → Take 1 day fully off. Reset. Restart smaller.

Don't double-down on a worse week.

## Calendar suggestions

### Treat your time blocks as appointments
- Add to your calendar
- "DSA Wed 9-10:30pm"
- Defend these blocks like meetings

### Theme days
- "Mondays = Algorithms"
- "Tuesdays = ML reading"
- "Wednesdays = System Design"
- "Thursdays = GenAI deep dive"
- "Fridays = Project"
- "Saturday = Mocks + Project"
- "Sunday = Mocks + Writing"

Reduces decision fatigue.

### Quiet hours
- Phone Do Not Disturb during prep blocks
- No Slack, no email
- Single-task only

## Accountability

Options for external accountability:

### A. Public commitment
- Tweet your weekly goals each Sunday
- "This week: 10 LC problems + 1 mock + 1 blog draft. Will report Friday."

### B. Buddy system
- Find another candidate prepping for FAANG
- Weekly check-in (15 min, Sunday)

### C. Paid coach
- 1 session/month with mentor
- $50-200/session
- They keep you honest

### D. Just track yourself
- Honest tracker can suffice IF you're consistent

For your profile (working full-time, Bengaluru, ambitious), I'd recommend **B + D** at minimum.

## What happens if you skip a Sunday review

Once: harmless. Make up for it Monday.

Two consecutive misses: red flag. Talk to someone. Take a day off. Recalibrate.

Three+ consecutive misses: you've lost the rhythm. Restart with smaller commitments to rebuild momentum.
