### Months 1-4 (Phase 1: Foundations)

End of each month:

- [ ] LinkedIn active (1+ post)

## Mid-roadmap adjustment protocol

At end of Month 6 + Month 12, decide:

### Are we on track?

**Signals you're on track:**
- LC-medium solvable in <30 min consistently
- 1-2 flagship projects shipped publicly
- Mock interview scores trending up
- Behavioral story bank developed
- Active LinkedIn / GitHub / blog presence

**Signals you're NOT on track:**
- Still struggling on LC-mediums >40 min
- 0 flagship projects shipped publicly
- No mock interview experience
- Resume V2 not yet finalized
- LinkedIn/GitHub stagnant

### If on track: stay the course

### If 1 month behind: catch up in 2 months via slight intensity boost (use weekends harder)

### If 2+ months behind: re-baseline the 15-month plan
- Move apply window to Sept/Oct/Nov 2027
- This is OK. Better to apply prepared than apply rushed.

### If 4+ months behind: consider re-thinking
- Are you actually working 25 hrs/week?
- Is something specific blocking progress?
- Talk to someone for outside perspective

## The "what would I tell next-year-me" exercise

End of each quarter (every 3 months), write a letter to your future self:

```
Dear Future Lourdu,

It's the end of [quarter]. Here's where I am:
- Current strengths: __
- Current weaknesses: __
- What I'm proud of: __
- What I regret not doing: __

What I want future-me to know:
- __

What I want future-me to do:
- __

If I could change one thing in the next 3 months: __

[signed]
Past Lourdu, [date]
```

These letters keep you tethered to your own narrative. Powerful when re-read 6 months later.

## Tools for tracking this

### Simple
- One markdown file per month in `09-the-grind/monthly/`
- Filename: `2026-06-review.md`

### Slightly more structured
- Notion page per month
- Linked to weekly logs

### Visual
- Excel sheet with charts (hours over time, problems solved, etc.)
- Public dashboard if you want accountability

Don't over-tool. The discipline is in DOING the review, not in the tool.

---

## Common failures of this practice

### Failure 1: Skipping reviews when behind
Counter: that's exactly when you most need them.

### Failure 2: Mechanical filling without reflection
Counter: actually think. If you don't have insights, you're not reflecting enough.

### Failure 3: Treating it as ceremonial
Counter: derive 1-3 concrete changes from each review. Implement them.

### Failure 4: Reading reviews to feel bad
Counter: balanced review covers wins + losses + lessons. Not flogging.
