# Code of conduct

Achilles is for learners at every level and from every background. We want asking a basic question here to feel as safe as reporting a subtle bug.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

**Expected**
- Be kind and assume good faith. Everyone was a beginner at every topic here once.
- Critique ideas, code and claims, never people. Back technical disagreement with evidence: a source, a test or a measurement.
- Credit other people's work, and be honest about what you have and have not verified.

**Not acceptable**
- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, accent, English, education or hardware budget.
- Sexualized language or imagery, threats, doxxing, or publishing private messages without consent.
- Plagiarism, or knowingly posting false technical claims.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space that represents the project. To report a problem, contact the maintainer ([@Lourdhu02](https://github.com/Lourdhu02)) privately through the email on their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature. Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, from a private warning up to a permanent ban.
