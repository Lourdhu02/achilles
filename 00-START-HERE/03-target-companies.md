## LEAGUE A — Realistic primary targets (60-70% probability of ≥1 offer)

These have India offices, hire 2-YOE laterally, and your GenAI portfolio maps to real teams.

### 1. Google (Bangalore / Hyderabad / Gurugram + US transfer path)
- **Role to target:** "Software Engineer, AI/ML" (L3/L4), "ML Engineer, GenAI" — Google Cloud Vertex AI team, Gemini Apps team, Search Generative Experience
- **Bar:** 5 rounds (1 phone screen, 4 onsite: 2 coding, 1 ML/system design, 1 behavioral, 1 hiring committee review afterwards)
- **Coding bar:** Hard. 2 LC-mediums or 1-medium + 1-hard in 45 min. Whiteboard-style on Google docs/Meet.
- **ML round:** "Design a system to detect harmful prompts at scale across Gemini" / "Walk me through how attention works"
- **Quirk:** Hiring committee is anonymous, evaluates packet *after* interviews. Less interviewer-impression, more "did your transcripts hit the bars". Optimize for clear written-quality answers.
- **Apply when:** Months 12-15 (after DSA is solid)
- **Referral angle:** Anyone you know who's been at Google 1+ year. Internal referral = direct recruiter contact, skip ATS.
- **Compensation (India L4 2026 estimates):** ₹50-75 LPA total
- **GenAI fit:** Very high. Gemini, Vertex AI Agent Builder, Project Astra.

### 2. Microsoft (Hyderabad / Bangalore / Noida + US transfer path)
- **Role to target:** "Senior ML Engineer / Software Engineer 2" (SDE-2 with ML focus), Microsoft Research India (more research-y), Azure AI / OpenAI integration teams
- **Bar:** 4-5 rounds. As-aas (a.k.a. STAR) heavy. Coding rounds use real IDE.
- **Coding bar:** Slightly easier than Google (LC-medium-leaning), but you must articulate clearly.
- **Quirk:** Microsoft loves "growth mindset" + "customer obsession" answers. Read Satya Nadella's "Hit Refresh" — referenced in onsite questions sometimes.
- **GenAI fit:** Very high. Microsoft owns ~49% of OpenAI; tons of integration roles, Copilot teams.
- **Apply when:** Months 10-14.
- **Referral angle:** Microsoft has internal referral program with bonuses — find a referrer.
- **Compensation (Hyd SDE-2 2026 estimates):** ₹45-65 LPA total

### 3. Nvidia (Bangalore / Pune / Hyderabad)
- **Role to target:** "Deep Learning Software Engineer", "AI Solutions Architect", "LLM Performance Engineer", "Triton Inference Server team"
- **Bar:** 4-6 rounds. Heavy on systems: CUDA basics, GPU memory hierarchy, attention kernels, FP8/INT8 quantization. Less LeetCode-heavy than Google.
- **Quirk:** They want people who understand HARDWARE. If you've never written a CUDA kernel, OK — but you should understand the *cost model*: HBM bandwidth, compute-bound vs memory-bound ops, FlashAttention motivation.
- **GenAI fit:** Extremely high. They power the entire industry.
- **Sweet spot for you:** Your TensorRT/ONNX-INT8 experience is unusually relevant.
- **Apply when:** Months 8-12 (can apply earlier — systems learning curve is steep but starts paying off fast).
- **Referral angle:** Nvidia Developer Programs, NGC catalog contributors, hackathons.
- **Compensation (Bangalore 2026 estimates):** ₹45-70 LPA

### 4. Adobe (Noida / Bangalore + San Jose)
- **Role to target:** "Machine Learning Engineer" on Firefly (image gen), Acrobat AI (document AI), Sensei platform
- **Bar:** 4-5 rounds. Reasonable. ML depth + system design + behavioral.
- **Quirk:** Adobe values aesthetic/UX intuition for creative AI. Your "user-facing OCR" experience plays well.
- **GenAI fit:** High (Firefly, GenAI for Creative Cloud).
- **Apply when:** Months 10-14.
- **Compensation (India 2026 estimates):** ₹35-55 LPA

### 5. Apple (Bangalore / Hyderabad + Cupertino)
- **Role to target:** "Machine Learning Engineer, Apple Intelligence", "Siri", "on-device ML"
- **Bar:** Hard. 4-6 rounds. Apple specifically values on-device, privacy-preserving ML. They like CoreML.
- **Quirk:** Apple is famously secretive. Job descriptions are vague. You won't know what team you're on until late stage.
- **GenAI fit:** High (Apple Intelligence launched 2024-2025, on-device LLM team is hiring).
- **Sweet spot for you:** ONNX quantization + edge deployment experience maps to on-device LLM.
- **Compensation (India 2026 estimates):** ₹50-70 LPA

### 6. Amazon (Bangalore / Hyderabad / Chennai)
- **Role to target:** "Applied Scientist II" (research-y), "SDE-2 ML" (engineering), Bedrock / Rufus / Alexa LLM teams
- **Bar:** 5 rounds. Each round mapped to 2-3 Leadership Principles. You will get behavioral questions in every round.
- **Quirk:** **Leadership Principles are everything.** Have 2 STAR stories per principle (32+ stories for 16 LPs). See `06-behavioral/02-amazon-LPs.md`.
- **GenAI fit:** High (Bedrock, Rufus shopping LLM, Alexa+ rewrite).
- **Apply when:** Months 9-13.
- **Compensation (India 2026 estimates):** SDE-2 ~₹35-50 LPA, Applied Scientist II ~₹45-70 LPA

### 7. Meta (Bangalore — small office, mostly recruiting)
- **Role to target:** "ML Engineer" on Llama, AI Studio, Reality Labs, Ranking. Note: Meta's India office is small; many roles require relocation.
- **Bar:** Very hard. 4 onsite rounds, all rigorous. 2 coding, 1 ML system design, 1 behavioral.
- **Quirk:** Meta uses "rooting interviews": you discuss your past projects in deep technical detail. Be ready to defend every claim on your resume.
- **GenAI fit:** Extreme. Llama is the flagship open-source LLM family.
- **Compensation (India / US 2026):** India ~₹50-75 LPA, US E4 ~$280-380k

### 8. Databricks (Bangalore + remote)
- **Role to target:** "Software Engineer, ML Platform", "Applied AI Engineer" on Mosaic AI
- **Bar:** 4-5 rounds. Strong systems focus (Spark/Delta), ML platform thinking.
- **Quirk:** Mosaic AI is their LLM bet (post-MosaicML acquisition). Lots of fine-tuning/training infra work.
- **GenAI fit:** Very high (Mosaic AI, DBRX model family, MLflow).
- **Compensation (India 2026):** Highly competitive, often beats FAANG India.

### 9. Atlassian (Bangalore + Sydney + remote)
- **Role to target:** "Senior Machine Learning Engineer", "AI Engineer" on Rovo / Atlassian Intelligence
- **Bar:** 4 rounds. Less LeetCode-grindy, more practical engineering judgment.
- **Quirk:** Values async communication, written quality. Their "Team Anywhere" remote-first culture is real.
- **GenAI fit:** High and growing.
- **Compensation (India 2026):** ₹45-60 LPA

### 10. Stripe (Bangalore office opening + remote)
- **Role to target:** "Machine Learning Engineer" on Radar (fraud), Foundation Models, Tax
- **Bar:** Very high. 5 rounds, all rigorous. Writing sample sometimes requested (Stripe's "writing culture").
- **Quirk:** Stripe asks deep, principled questions. Less pattern-matching, more "why does this design choice exist?"
- **GenAI fit:** Moderate (more classical ML + LLM applications), but their bar attracts top talent.
- **Compensation (Bangalore/remote 2026):** ₹50-80 LPA

## LEAGUE B — Stretch targets (15-25% probability)

Smaller, harder, more global competition. No India offices for most.

### B1. Anthropic (San Francisco + London + remote-applied)
- **Role to target:** "Member of Technical Staff, Applied AI", "Forward Deployed Engineer", "ML Engineer, Inference"
- **Bar:** Extremely high. 5-7 rounds. Heavy on alignment thinking, AI safety concepts.
- **Quirk:** Anthropic interviews include a "values" round. You must articulate why AI safety matters to YOU and what you'd refuse to build. See `06-behavioral/03-anthropic-fit.md`.
- **Remote angle:** Some roles are open globally. Watch carefully.
- **GenAI fit:** Perfect, but the bar is absolute. You'll be competing against Stanford PhDs and ex-Google staff engineers.
- **Compensation (2026 estimates):** $300-500k+ for senior roles, USD

### B2. OpenAI (San Francisco + remote-applied limited)
- **Role to target:** "Member of Technical Staff", "Applied Engineer" on ChatGPT, API, Agents
- **Bar:** Brutal. 5-8 rounds.
- **Quirk:** OpenAI values "ship velocity" — you'll be asked about products you've shipped, why, how fast. Founder experience helps you here.
- **Remote angle:** Limited but growing.
- **Compensation (2026):** $400-700k+ for MTS, USD + PPUs

### B3. Cohere (Toronto + remote)
- **Role to target:** "Member of Technical Staff", "ML Engineer" on Command R/R+, RAG team
- **Bar:** High but more open than Anthropic/OpenAI. Hires globally remote.
- **GenAI fit:** Perfect — they are an enterprise RAG/LLM company; your FinSentinelAI work is directly relevant.
- **Compensation (2026):** $200-350k USD/CAD equivalent

### B4. Hugging Face (Paris + remote-global)
- **Role to target:** "ML Engineer", "Open Source Engineer", "TRL team", "Inference Endpoints team"
- **Bar:** High but unconventional. Heavy weight on open-source contributions.
- **Quirk:** **They look at your HF profile + GitHub before your resume.** If you become an HF community heavyweight (popular Space, popular model, popular dataset, popular blog) — you'll get reached out to.
- **Strategy:** Spend 3 hours/week on HF community building from Month 3 onwards.
- **Compensation (2026):** $150-300k+ remote

### B5. Scale AI (San Francisco + remote selective)
- **Role to target:** "ML Engineer" on GenAI services, evaluation, agents
- **Bar:** Hard. 5 rounds.
- **GenAI fit:** Very high (data labeling for top labs + agent platforms).

### B6. xAI (San Francisco + remote selective)
- **Role to target:** "Software Engineer, ML", "Inference Engineer"
- **Bar:** Hard. Less transparent process.
- **Quirk:** Move-fast culture; favors hackers over researchers.

### B7. Mistral AI (Paris + remote)
- **Role to target:** "Research Engineer", "Applied AI Engineer"
- **Bar:** Hard. European-focused but global.

## YOUR APPLICATION ORDER (apply in this sequence — don't apply to everything at once)

Each application is interview practice. Burn lower-stakes applications first.

**Wave 1 (Month 10-11) — Practice league** (apply, even if borderline; treat as real interview reps):
- Stripe India (high bar, great practice)
- Atlassian (moderate bar, learn writing-heavy interviews)
- Adobe (moderate bar)

**Wave 2 (Month 12-13) — Core league A:**
- Microsoft (warmest entry)
- Amazon (lots of openings, LP-heavy practice)
- Nvidia (your TensorRT story plays well)
- Databricks

**Wave 3 (Month 13-14) — Top League A:**
- Google
- Apple
- Meta

**Wave 4 (Month 14-15) — Stretch (League B):**
- Cohere, Hugging Face (remote)
- Anthropic, OpenAI (applied)
- xAI, Mistral, Scale

**Why this order:** Earlier-wave interviews are reps. By Wave 4, you're an interview machine. Mistakes you'd make at Anthropic in Month 11 won't happen by Month 15.
